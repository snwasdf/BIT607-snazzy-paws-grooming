You can use this website to view services, check prices, contact us, and book grooming appointments!

Dog Care Advice

We also offer dog care advice that you can do at home between appointments!

  • Fresh water in clean bowls must always be available for your dog.
  • Make sure your dog has insurance.
  • Daily exercise and mental stimulation can help prevent behavioural and health problems with your dog.
  • Make sure your dog has a healthy and balanced diet.
